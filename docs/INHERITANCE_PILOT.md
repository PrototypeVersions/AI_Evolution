At the final checkpoint the continuous learner led. The three arms approached saturation on these small tasks; inheritance did not show a clear advantage over fresh offspring. Two-operation instructions remained near chance under this architecture and instruction encoding. That probe has an underdetermined instruction convention and does not establish a general inability to compose skills.

The pilot motivated a fixed 384-round stopping window for the official chapter. It did not motivate removing the continuous learner or changing seeds to make inheritance win. These are preliminary descriptive observations.
