# Research plan

## Current milestone

Establish a transparent baseline laboratory with deterministic replay, complete trial provenance, explicit controls, uncertainty across independent worlds, and a usable visual interface. The first campaign asks whether an abrupt environmental change reduces the benefit of faithful inherited memory.

## Next experiments

1. Vary change severity and speed. Compare stable, partial, gradual, cyclic, and abrupt changes without revising hypotheses after inspecting confirmation data.
2. Add memory decay and energetic cost. Ask when retaining information is worth its resource cost, and whether forgetting improves recovery.
3. Test controller dependence. Repeat with a different learning rule and compare inherited memory with inherited policy parameters.
4. Test apparent roles through interventions. Establish prospective behavioral definitions, examine generalization, then remove or exchange candidate roles while controlling population size and resources.
5. Add actual LLM-controlled agents only after these baselines are sound. Treat model identity, prompts, context, sampling settings, actual outputs, cost, and version as experimental variables. API sampling cannot be promised bit-for-bit reproducible.

## Automated research scope

The current automation runs a fixed protocol on fresh seed blocks and reports its outcomes. It does not invent hypotheses, modify its own code, or autonomously certify discoveries. The daily workflow has a finite budget and saves negative as well as positive outcomes.

A later experiment director may select from a declared experiment vocabulary. Exploration data can guide selection, but fresh confirmation data and a frozen protocol must remain separate. A future GPT research assistant should be able to propose experiments and summarize evidence without editing completed records or changing the simulator during an active test.

## Publication preparation

Complete a primary-source review of digital evolution, artificial life, cultural transmission, nonstationary learning, and causal accounts of emergent organization. Identify what existing work already establishes. A claim about this model must be distinguished from a general claim about artificial intelligence or a philosophical conclusion about higher-level properties.

Archive code and complete data independently of temporary workflow storage. Keep a claim-to-evidence ledger that includes counterexamples and failed replications. Seek results that survive changes in modeling assumptions and permit explanatory interventions rather than relying only on visually compelling dynamics.
