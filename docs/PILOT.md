# Pilot calibration before the first campaign

Date: 2026-10-12. Pilot seed: 1729, excluded from the controlled research seed blocks.

An initial pilot used 3.8 energy per successful harvest, 0.55 upkeep, lifetime 60, and reproduction cost 15. All three memory conditions filled almost every location. Post-change occupancy was approximately 99.0%, 98.9%, and 98.6% for no inheritance, faithful, and shuffled memory. The ceiling would obscure meaningful population differences.

Before running the controlled campaign, successful harvest reward was reduced to 3.0, upkeep increased to 0.70, lifetime reduced to 50, and reproduction cost increased to 17. A second pilot at the same seed had occupancy 89.3%, 81.4%, and 49.1%, respectively. These parameters were retained; no further parameter search was performed.

The primary question and negative interaction prediction were proposed before the pilots, but the pilot outcomes were inspected while calibrating the environment. Consequently the first campaign is a controlled development-stage experiment, not a blind test of an untouched model selected independently of outcomes. The fixed protocol was saved locally before the 24 exploration and 48 confirmation seed blocks were run.

The known difference between useful and corrupted memories is an intentional treatment. Novelty, surprise, and external validity must not be inferred merely from the resulting contrasts.
