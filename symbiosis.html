    05 / KNOW WHAT YOU’RE WATCHING

    Real records. Explicit limits.

    Read the full protocol ↗
    01

    Programs can change shape.

    Offspring inherit decision circuits. Mutation can add, remove, rewire or retune units. Experience updates action values; a fraction can pass to offspring.

    02

    Other lives change the game.

    Food and mineral have different uses. Agents can exchange surpluses, build lasting structures, raid neighbors and defend. Every action has a cost.

    03

    This world is preserved.

    The schedule is paused. Its checkpoint, replay and releases remain available as a separate artificial-life experiment. Visitors can inspect the records.

    04

    Complexity is not intelligence.

    This is a bounded artificial ecology: 256 sites, nine actions and up to 48 circuit units. Longer programs may reflect mutation bias. No claim of unlimited growth or general intelligence is made.

    A new experiment, with its own beginning. Symbiosis 002 follows Continuum 001. The original world’s checkpoint and records are preserved, with its schedule paused.
